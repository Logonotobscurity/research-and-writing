GroundedGuide was discussed as a knowledge/guide product. The user's stated goal was to reach a comparable capability level to a private reference product, while ensuring public positioning and repository descriptions express GroundedGuide's own identity and capabilities and do not expose the reference as a reverse-engineering source.

Other knowledge-related concepts include Curriculum LMS, NLP Compendium, ToolScout, Tool Recommendation RAG Engine, and LOG_ON Prompt Pattern Library. Their status varies and should be verified individually.

## 6. Interpersonal and short-form writing

The retained recent conversation references include brief requests for help responding to personal messages, including flirtatious tone. Treat these as context-sensitive drafting tasks, not as evidence of relationship status or private circumstances.

The same drafting discipline applies: understand the relationship and intended tone, avoid inventing facts or emotional commitments, keep the message natural, and preserve the user's voice. Not every short interpersonal message needs to explicitly use Identity-Tension Writing.

## 7. Verification before external presentation

For each creative, media, or knowledge product, confirm the owned repository, maturity, end-to-end workflow, licences, source-ingestion limits, platform API rules, accessibility, and whether it is a prototype or live service. Avoid disclosing private reference methods or implying third-party work is owned.
