## NotebookLM frameworks context

A long pasted document (compiled 14 Oct 2026) summarised ~25 newsletter threads on NotebookLM / Gemini Notebook use cases. Relevant patterns extracted for this task:

- Narrow, focused notebooks beat broad ones.
- Grounding rules (“Not in source”) increase trustworthiness.
- Structure + style + art-direction prompts improve visual outputs.
- Human judgment remains essential; verify claims.
- Chain tools by role (NotebookLM for grounded research, other tools for execution/cleanup).

These informed the decision to produce both written lines and ready-to-generate flyer prompts.

Research was executed; 8 lines with mechanisms and contexts were generated; 8 vertical flyer images were produced and returned.
