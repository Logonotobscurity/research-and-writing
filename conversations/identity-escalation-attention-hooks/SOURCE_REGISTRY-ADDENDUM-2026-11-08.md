## D. Citation and provenance requirements for future entries

1. One URL per source entry; do not bundle sources behind “multiple.”
2. Preserve the exact URL returned or opened, preferably with its canonical or DOI form.
3. Distinguish primary research from reviews, practitioner articles, and marketing commentary.
4. Connect each source to the specific claim, line, or section it supports.
5. Record whether the source was opened during the original conversation or added later.
6. Preserve access dates and publication dates separately.
7. If a source is used for a factual claim, retain the in-text citation or footnote in the deliverable itself.
8. When the exact original source cannot be recovered, state that plainly instead of inferring a URL from an author name or citation fragment.
