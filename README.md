# Research & Writing

A durable notebook for research, writing, conceptual development, and the evolution of ideas across conversations.

## Editorial principles

- Preserve the development of an idea, not only its final formulation.
- Distinguish **user statements**, **assistant interpretations**, **joint conclusions**, and **open hypotheses**.
- Never present a reconstructed summary as a verbatim transcript.
- Keep uncertainty visible. Separate verified facts, reported claims, inference, hypothesis, assumption, and unknown.
- Record disagreements and revisions rather than silently overwriting earlier positions.
- Use evidence → hypotheses → tests → conclusions when developing research.
- Treat each conversation as a self-contained folder when it forms a distinct line of inquiry.

## Archive status

The conversation folders document phases and conclusions using the source context available at the time of writing. They are structured research records, **not complete raw exports of every message**. Synthesized passages are labelled as such. Future additions should preserve exact message text when available and append new dated phases rather than rewriting the historical record.
